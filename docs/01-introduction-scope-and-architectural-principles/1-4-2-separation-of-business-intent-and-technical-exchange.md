# 1.4.2 Separation of Business Intent and Technical Exchange

Business objectives explain why information must move and what result is useful to the participants. A MAP transaction explains the technical shared activity used to move or process information. Multiple transactions may support one objective, and one reusable transaction may serve many objectives.

Consequently, transaction identifiers, message identifiers, workflow-unit identifiers, and business-context identifiers must not be collapsed into a single notion merely because they are related.

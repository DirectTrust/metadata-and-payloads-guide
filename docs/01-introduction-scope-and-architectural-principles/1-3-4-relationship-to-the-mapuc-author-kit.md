# 1.3.4 Relationship to the MAPUC Author Kit

The MAPUC Author Kit provides a repeatable pathway for authors: gathering business and technical inputs, selecting and adapting reusable TX Definitions, drafting IG chapters, applying templates, and performing review and QA. It is a governance and production aid for specifications.

Author Kit rules about file naming, document structure, or author workflow must not silently become runtime obligations for Sender or Receiver actors. Such obligations must be explicitly established in the Framework, an applicable TX Definition, or a use-case IG.

# 1.4.1 Two-Party Information Exchange

A MAP transaction represents a shared activity between a Sender and a Receiver. Other systems may convey, transform, or support the exchange, but their involvement does not erase the primary Sender/Receiver perspective of the transaction. A chain of technical hops should not automatically be mistaken for a chain of distinct business transactions.

Where an intermediary performs a separately specified shared activity, that activity can be represented by its own transaction and responsibilities.

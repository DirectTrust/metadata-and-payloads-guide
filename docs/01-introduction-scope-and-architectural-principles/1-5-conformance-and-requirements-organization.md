# 1.5 Conformance and Requirements Organization

Conformance is determined by the requirements applicable to a particular actor, transaction, and use case—not by every statement in every MAP artifact. Readers should identify the responsible actor, the triggering condition, the required behavior, and the specification layer that establishes the obligation.

Use of SHALL, SHOULD, and MAY is intended to convey normative strength where the statement is expressly framed as a requirement. Informative rationale, examples, and authoring instructions must be distinguished from exchange obligations.

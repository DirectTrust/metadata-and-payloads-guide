# 1.3.5 RTC Governance and the Reuse Feedback Cycle

The RTC is the governed home of GenTXDefs, not a repository of every UCTXDef published by every use case. Its governance function includes reviewing proposed generalized patterns, preserving transaction identity and common semantics, controlling revisions, and making approved definitions available for reuse.

Use-case development provides practical evidence about where generalized choices are sufficient and where new patterns are necessary. A newly published UCTXDef may be submitted for review, but the governing body determines whether it should become a new GenTXDef, lead to a revision of an existing GenTXDef, or remain specific to its originating use case. The decision should preserve traceability from the source UCTXDef to the catalog outcome.

Catalog acceptance does not retroactively change the published UC IG. Adoption of a new or revised GenTXDef by an existing IG requires an explicit maintenance decision under that IG's governance.

# 1.4.5 Preservation of Information Identity, Context, and Provenance

Exchange should preserve the identity of the information object, its relevant clinical or business context, and the provenance assertions received with it when the applicable rules require preservation. A new transport event does not automatically create a new authored clinical document, and relaying content does not make the relay its original author.

A system may authoritatively assert activities it performed or reliably observed within its purview. It should not manufacture first-hand assertions about activities known only to other systems. Chapter 3.10 and Appendix E distinguish recording, preserving, and reporting provenance; a TX Definition establishes any specific exchange-reporting obligation.

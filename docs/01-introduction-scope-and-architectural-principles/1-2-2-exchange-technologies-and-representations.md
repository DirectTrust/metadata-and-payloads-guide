# 1.2.2 Exchange Technologies and Representations

Direct provides the core transport context. Depending on the applicable TX Definition, edge systems may use XD-style metadata and payload packages or FHIR representations, including document and collection patterns. CDA documents, HL7 V2 messages, FHIR resources, PDFs, images, and other media may be exchanged where permitted by the transaction and use case.

A representation choice does not change the underlying responsibilities for identifying the information, preserving relevant human-readable content, or honoring the applicable processing rules. Detailed transport, metadata, and representation constraints are addressed in Chapters 3–5 and supporting appendices.

# 1.2.4 What the MAP Framework Does Not Specify

The common Framework does not define a particular business use case, mandate a particular set of transactions for every implementation, author clinical document templates, or claim that every transport intermediary can observe or attest every downstream processing event.

It does not independently establish consent, legal authorization, trustworthiness of clinical assertions, or conformance testing infrastructure. Such matters may be governed by other specifications or policies and may be referenced where applicable.

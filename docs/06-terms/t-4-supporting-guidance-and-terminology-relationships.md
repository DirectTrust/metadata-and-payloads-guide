# T.4 Supporting Guidance and Terminology Relationships

This section directs readers to the specifications that establish implementation requirements and to supporting material that illustrates the terminology. Definitions establish meaning; they do not silently create new obligations or supersede the governing standards.

## T.4.1 Relationship to Normative Requirements

Chapter 3 establishes common transaction architecture and technical requirements. Governed Generalized Transaction Definitions (GenTXDefs) in the Reusable Transaction Catalog (RTC) and the applicable Use-Case Transaction Definitions (UCTXDefs) specify additional or constrained requirements. Terms defined here do not turn conditional reporting or use-case-specific processing into universal requirements.

## T.4.2 Relationship to Vocabulary and Code Systems

The vocabulary chapter and applicable terminology packages identify code systems, value sets, and bindings. A conceptual term in Chapter T does not by itself establish a binding, cardinality, or encoded field requirement. Authors must use exact terminology and field names when establishing conformance rules.

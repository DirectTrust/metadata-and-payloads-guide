# Business Actors Framework

The following business actor Roles can be used to describe Business Actors in a Scenario. This is not a complete list of Business Actors, but these business actor roles will help you describe Business Actors.

<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 27%" />
<col style="width: 72%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>Business Actor</strong></td>
<td><strong>Description</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Sending Entity</td>
<td>The entity that is the subject of the Certificate associated with the Direct address that is initiating the Direct message. (The From address.)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Receiving Entity</td>
<td>The entity that is the subject of the Certificate associated with the Direct address that is receiving the Direct message. (The To address.)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Sending Organization</td>
<td>An organization that utilizes Direct messaging to send information to another Entity operating on the DirectTrust Network.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Receiving Organization</td>
<td>An organization that utilizes Direct messaging to receive information from another Entity operating on the DirectTrust Network.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Sending End User</td>
<td>Personnel or devices employed by an organization who utilize Direct messaging to send information to another Entity operating on the DirectTrust Network.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Receiving End User</td>
<td>Personnel or devices employed by an organization who utilize Direct messaging to receive information from another Entity operating on the DirectTrust Network.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Intermediary</td>
<td>An organization that provides extended health information services for Direct Secure Messaging.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Health Information Service Provider (HISP)</td>
<td>An organization authorized to operate as a Health Information Services Provider under a policy authority like DirectTrust.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Certificate Authority</td>
<td><p>An authority trusted by one or more users to create and assign</p>
<p>certificates. Frequently abbreviated as CA.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Registration Authority</td>
<td><p>The entity responsible for identification and authentication of certificate</p>
<p>Subjects frequently abbreviated as RA. RA’s may also perform identity proofing for non-PKI credentials issued by Identity Providers. RA’s have the capability to identity proof organizations, practitioners, and Individuals.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Identity Provider (IdP)</td>
<td>An Identity Provider issues non-PKI credentials and manages authenticators and the credential life-cycle for duly identity proofed subjects. The IdP can authenticate previously identity proofed individual with one or more authenticators. The IdP offers authorization services which can be used in a tiered OAuth scenario to simplify user authentication.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Credential Service Provider (CSP)</td>
<td>NIST calls organizations that perform both the RA and IdP function Credential Service Providers.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

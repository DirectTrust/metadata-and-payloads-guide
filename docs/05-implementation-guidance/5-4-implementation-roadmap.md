# 5.4 Implementation Roadmap

Begin with the Shared Activity and identify or develop its applicable Use-Case Transaction Definition. Identify participants and grouped Sender Actor and Receiver Actor responsibilities; resolve an authorized Routing Target and the receiving endpoint’s declared capabilities; prepare the information objects, SubmissionSet/List identity and purpose, applicable Context, metadata, and Provenance, and a Workflow Activity Task when required or elected. Package and transmit the Direct message, evaluate the structured payload, and perform the accepted work. Correlated workflow-status reporting applies only when required by the applicable Transaction Definition.

For applicable Direct message packaging, MIME Part 1 provides the required text/plain human-readable communication and any optional HTML alternative; MIME Part 2 carries the structured payload with corresponding human-readable copies under Chapter 3.

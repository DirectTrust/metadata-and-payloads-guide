# 5.7.1 AI-Assisted Content Creators and Content Consumers

AI assistance may support the grouped Sender Actor in classifying source information, selecting permitted information-object representations, preparing required metadata and terminology, assembling a Workflow Activity Task when required or elected, associating Context Instances, creating XD or FHIR metadata, and packaging a Direct message. The Content Creator establishes at least the minimum Information Source information needed to initiate the transaction; the grouped Sender Actor completes the required information objects and Provenance and any applicable Task. The SubmissionSet business identifier and purpose remain required independently of Task inclusion. The division of work between Content Creator and Message Sender may vary by implementation. AI assistance does not relax transaction requirements.

AI-assisted Content Consumers may use explicit exchange metadata to identify the Shared Activity, patient, Context, and requested workflow, while interpreting the included information objects. Narrative notes, summaries, birth plans, referrals, patient-authored information, and source forms can support processing without requiring the sender to pre-code every statement. Derived interpretations remain attributable to the processing system rather than being presented as original source assertions.

Use of AI does not alter the externally observable conformance requirements of a transaction, the capabilities asserted by an endpoint, or the obligation to preserve accurate identity, authority, provenance, and accountability.

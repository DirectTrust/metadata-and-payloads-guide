# 3.9.8 Missing Workflow Status and Follow Up

When workflow status reporting is requested or otherwise required and an applicable reporting interval is established, the expected interval SHALL start when the last Sender Actor acting within the Information Source Organization’s scope of control sends to a Routing Target associated with the Information Recipient Organization. Source-side preparation and intermediary hops before that handoff are outside the interval. Recipient-side routing, receipt, evaluation, and return of the workflow status report are within it; additional recipient-side hops SHALL NOT restart the clock. The default interval for this use case is 60 minutes unless an explicit use-case or Information Recipient-specific interval is established. Processing after acceptance is outside the reporting interval. No TX8 timeout obligation arises solely from the presence of an optional Task.

A failure to dispatch TX8 because no eligible destination could be resolved SHALL be distinguished from TX8 that was dispatched but subsequently failed during message delivery or downstream processing. A missing TX8 Workflow Status Update SHALL NOT, by itself, be interpreted as evidence that the originating Transaction Instance was rejected or unsuccessfully processed. The receiving system SHALL NOT initiate another TX8 solely to report the failure to dispatch TX8 Workflow Status Update. TX8 SHALL terminate workflow-status chaining.

If no correlated TX8 is received within the applicable reporting interval, the originating system SHALL close its transaction tracking context with the outcome unconfirmed and mark it as requiring follow-up. This administrative closure does not establish acceptance, rejection, or non-receipt and does not cancel required reporting. A late TX8 SHALL be correlated to the original transaction and used to resolve its recorded unconfirmed outcome; it SHALL NOT create a new originating Transaction Instance or erase the timeout and follow-up history. Missing or unreadable correlation information or a missing return endpoint may prevent a correlated report; the timeout does not identify the cause. It SHALL NOT require automatic retransmission.

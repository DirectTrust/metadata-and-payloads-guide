# 3.9.1 Structured Payload Acceptance Reporting

Structured payload acceptance SHALL be reported through TX8 when reporting is required by the applicable Transaction Definition or an applicable workflow-status reporting obligation and the sender and receiving endpoint support the required TX8 roles. Where TX8 reporting is permitted but not required, it MAY be sent when the requisite endpoint capabilities and routing are established. Absence of a reporting request does not prohibit otherwise permitted reporting. Presence of a Workflow Activity Task alone SHALL NOT make TX8 reporting mandatory.

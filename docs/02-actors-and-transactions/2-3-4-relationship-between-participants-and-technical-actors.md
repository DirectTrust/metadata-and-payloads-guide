# 2.3.4 Relationship Between Participants and Technical Actors

Business parties and transaction participants express **who is responsible for the shared activity**; technical actors express **which systems perform technical functions**; provenance agents express **who or what participated in an asserted historical activity**. These lenses may refer to related entities but are not interchangeable.

The technical sender of a message may act on behalf of a different responsible organization, and the document's original author may be neither the sender nor the receiving system.

# 2.5 Transaction Interactions and Information Flow

Information may flow through a single transaction or a sequence of related transactions. The sequence should preserve distinctions between business intent, the transaction activity, the message or messages used, the structured payload, and any resulting status reports.

This concept is part of the reusable MAP architecture; its detailed exchange obligations arise only where the Framework and an applicable transaction specification expressly assign them. In a UC IG, use-case choices are documented in the relevant UCTXDef rather than inferred from illustrative architecture.

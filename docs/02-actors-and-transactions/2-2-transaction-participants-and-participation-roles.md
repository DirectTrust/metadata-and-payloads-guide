# 2.2 Transaction Participants and Participation Roles

A **transaction participant** is a party occupying a role in the shared activity represented by a MAP transaction. Participation is defined relative to that transaction, not as a permanent characteristic of the organization or system. The same organization may be Sender in one transaction and Receiver in another.

This concept is part of the reusable MAP architecture; its detailed exchange obligations arise only where the Framework and an applicable transaction specification expressly assign them. In a UC IG, use-case choices are documented in the relevant UCTXDef rather than inferred from illustrative architecture.

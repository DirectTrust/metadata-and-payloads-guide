# 2.5 Transaction Interactions and Information Flow

The MAP Use Case Framework relates business outcomes to coordinated exchanges. **Information Exchange Objectives** state the outcomes to support; **Business Scenarios** describe the circumstances, triggers, and conditions under which those outcomes must be achieved. **Responsible Organizations** identify the participating parties and their capacities. Analysis of each scenario then identifies **Required Information Exchanges**: the Shared Activities, information, participants, and sequencing necessary to accomplish the objective.

Only after these requirements are established does **RTC Analysis** identify which GenTXDefs can be reused and constrained as UCTXDefs, which new UCTXDefs must be authored, and which activities remain external or placeholders. The resulting transactions may be solicited or unsolicited, combined in multi-transaction workflows, and implemented across multiple technical actors. The subsections explain these interaction patterns without making illustrative models into conformance requirements.

The MAPUC Author Kit's Chapter 2 Workbook supports this progression. Its Objective-to-Transaction Matrix and Actor Transaction Diagrams are architectural outputs documenting the traceability and interactions established by the analysis.

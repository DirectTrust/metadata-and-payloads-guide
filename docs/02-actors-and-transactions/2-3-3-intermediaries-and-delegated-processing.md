# 2.3.3 Intermediaries and Delegated Processing

An intermediary may route, relay, package, transform, or otherwise process information according to its role and agreements. Its capabilities and visibility are bounded: it can attest actions it performed or reliably observed, but cannot be required by the common Framework to report every activity occurring in other systems.

Requirements to generate or communicate particular provenance assertions must be expressly stated in the applicable TX Definition. Preserving another system's assertion is different from creating a new first-hand assertion.

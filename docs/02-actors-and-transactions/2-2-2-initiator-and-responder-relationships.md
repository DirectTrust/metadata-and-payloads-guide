# 2.2.2 Initiator and Responder Relationships

A request may initiate a business interaction that later produces one or more response transactions. Initiator and responder relationships explain the conversational or workflow context; they do not necessarily mean that the same transaction instance continues across every subsequent message.

A responding transaction has its own applicable identity and correlation requirements. A solicited response must identify its relationship to the originating request as required by the TX Definition.
